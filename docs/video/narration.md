# ESP Six-scene Film

## One request. Too many places to look.

Can we introduce this software? An employee wants an outcome, not another portal. Enterprise Skill Platform connects that request to the right capability, with governance built in.

## Reuse capability, not copied logic.

Different agents often repeat retrieval, checks, and reporting. ESP proposes a different unit of reuse: one governed skill, serving many experiences. Its contract keeps versions, permissions, and evidence requirements together.

## One path. Shared controls.

Follow the request: discover an eligible skill, check permissions, and invoke its approved plugin. Retain evidence before a decision. Discovery is not execution, and an automated check is not human approval.

The complete review retains a human decision and its limited scope. A separate missing-evidence review blocks approval. These are existing records, not a new approval made for this video. The development identity is shared.
